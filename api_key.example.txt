To turn on the SmartVolt AI agents and chat:
1. Get a FREE key at https://console.groq.com (it starts with gsk_)
2. Rename this file to api_key.txt
3. Replace the line below with your key, then restart the app

gsk_XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX

The app also works without a key (it uses built-in explanations instead).
api_key.txt is listed in .gitignore, so your real key is never uploaded.
