About SmartVolt

SmartVolt forecasts day-ahead electricity prices for the Spanish market with an N-BEATSx deep learning model, then a team of AI agents turns the forecast into simple money-saving advice: the best time to run your appliances, charge your car, and use your battery.

How the AI agents work

A Supervisor agent coordinates the team. A Forecast agent runs the N-BEATSx model to predict the next 24 hours of prices with a risk band. A Recommendation agent turns the forecast into the cheapest times to use electricity and the money saved. An Explanation agent explains it in plain words and answers your questions.

Important

SmartVolt is built for the Spanish market, because the model is trained on Spanish data. The advice is an estimate to help you save money, not financial advice, and no real trades are made on your behalf.
